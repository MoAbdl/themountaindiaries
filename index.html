The Story

It started with a question that would not leave. The kind that waits before dawn: what are you actually made of?

The mountains do not lift us. They simply remove everything that is not truly us.

The first mountain answered nothing. It took breath, certainty, and every assumption I arrived with.

This is a record of what surrounds the summits. Wrong turns, unexpected companions, mornings of light and ice.

Ama Dablam from the Kongma La trail, Khumbu
